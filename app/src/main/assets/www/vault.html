Evidence vault

Keep proof safe, and prove it hasn’t changed.

Save screenshots, files, links and notes in an encrypted vault that never leaves your phone. Every file gets a fingerprint, and you can export a clear report for NCCIA or FIA.

Loading…

Good evidence includes

  • The username, profile link and phone number involved
  • Date and time visible in each screenshot
  • The full conversation, not only the worst message
  • Payment details or account numbers they asked for

Avoid

  • Editing, cropping or annotating the original
  • Deleting chats or the account before saving
  • Sharing the material with others “to prove it”
  • Paying or negotiating to get it removed
How it protects youItems are encrypted with AES-256 using your passphrase, stored only on this phone, and locked automatically. If you forget the passphrase nobody can recover them. Anyone who knows it, or who can watch you type it, can open the vault.
