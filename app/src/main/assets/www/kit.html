Ambassador kit

Run a session on your campus.

A ready workshop, talking points, posters, QR codes and an event sign-up. Everything works without internet, so you can run it anywhere.

60-minute workshop plan

Aim for 15 to 40 people. Keep the room calm and supportive.

Ground rules

  • What is said in the room stays in the room.
  • No one has to share a personal story.
  • Never blame the person who was targeted.
  • Anyone may step out at any time.
  • Have a counsellor or teacher present if you can.

Talking points

  • Blackmail and image abuse are crimes, and the fault is never the victim’s.
  • Save evidence before blocking or deleting.
  • Never share an OTP or send money to settle a threat.
  • It affects every gender and age.
  • Help is free: 1799, 9911, 1043, 0800-39393.

Printable posters

Print on A4 or save as PDF. Each carries the helplines and an Urdu line.
