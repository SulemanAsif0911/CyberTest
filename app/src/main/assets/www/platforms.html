Platform guides

How to report, block and remove content.

Step-by-step reporting for the apps people use most, plus ready-to-paste messages. Save your evidence first, then report.

Save evidence before you block or reportBlocking or reporting can hide the account or the content from you. Use the evidence vault first.

Choose an app

Tap an app to see how to report content, impersonation and intimate images, and how to lock down your account.

InInstagram

Report a profile or content

  1. Open the profile, tap the ⋯ menu, then Report.
  2. Choose the closest reason, for example Pretending to be someone or Harassment or bullying.
  3. Follow the prompts and submit. You can also report an individual message by pressing and holding it.

Someone pretending to be you

  1. Open the profile, tap ⋯, choose Report → Pretending to be someone, then say who it is pretending to be.

Intimate images or blackmail

  1. Report the photo or message and choose the option for nudity or sexual content shared without consent, or for blackmail.

Block and protect your account

  1. Profile ⋯ → Block. In Settings, set your account to Private and limit who can message or tag you.

Menus change between app versions. If you can’t find an option, search the Help Centre for “report impersonation” or “report intimate images”. Open Instagram Help Centre ↗

FaFacebook

Report a profile or content

  1. Open the profile or post, tap ⋯ (or the three dots), then Find support or report profile.
  2. Choose the reason that fits, such as Pretending to be someone or Harassment.
  3. Submit, and check your Support Inbox for updates.

Someone pretending to be you

  1. Profile ⋯ → Find support or report → Pretending to be someone, then choose who is being impersonated.

Intimate images or blackmail

  1. Report the post, photo or video and choose the option about intimate images shared without permission.

Block and protect your account

  1. Profile ⋯ → Block. In Settings → Privacy, limit who can see your posts and find you.

Menus change between app versions. If you can’t find an option, search the Help Centre for “report impersonation” or “report intimate images”. Open Facebook Help Centre ↗

WhWhatsApp

Report a profile or content

  1. Open the chat, tap ⋯ (or the contact name), then Report.
  2. You can choose to include recent messages, then Report and block.
  3. To report a single message, press and hold it and choose Report.

Someone pretending to be you

  1. Report the number from the chat. Tell your contacts not to reply to it, and post a short notice on your real accounts.

Intimate images or blackmail

  1. Save evidence first (screenshots, number, dates), then report and block. Do not forward the images, even to prove a point.

Block and protect your account

  1. Settings → Privacy: set Profile photo, About and Last seen to “My contacts”, and turn on two-step verification.

Menus change between app versions. If you can’t find an option, search the Help Centre for “report impersonation” or “report intimate images”. Open WhatsApp Help Centre ↗

SnSnapchat

Report a profile or content

  1. Press and hold the Snap, Story or the person’s name, then tap Report.
  2. Choose the reason, add a note if asked, and submit.
  3. Block them from their profile (⋯ → Block) once you have saved the evidence.

Someone pretending to be you

  1. Open their profile, tap ⋯ → Report Account and pick the impersonation option.

Intimate images or blackmail

  1. Report the Snap or Story and pick the option for nudity or sexual content or for threats and blackmail.

Block and protect your account

  1. Settings → Who Can…: set Contact Me and View My Story to “My Friends”. Turn on Ghost Mode for the map.

Menus change between app versions. If you can’t find an option, search the Help Centre for “report impersonation” or “report intimate images”. Open Snapchat Help Centre ↗

TiTikTok

Report a profile or content

  1. Open the video or profile, tap Share → Report (or ⋯ → Report).
  2. Choose the reason that fits, then submit.
  3. For messages, press and hold the message, then Report.

Someone pretending to be you

  1. Profile ⋯ → Report → Pretending to be someone.

Intimate images or blackmail

  1. Report the video and choose the option for nudity or sexual activity, or for harassment and bullying.

Block and protect your account

  1. Settings → Privacy: set account to Private, limit comments, Duets and messages to friends.

Menus change between app versions. If you can’t find an option, search the Help Centre for “report impersonation” or “report intimate images”. Open TikTok Help Centre ↗

XX (Twitter)

Report a profile or content

  1. Open the post or profile, tap ⋯, then Report.
  2. Choose It’s abusive or harmful or the closest reason, then follow the steps.
  3. Mute or block the account after saving evidence.

Someone pretending to be you

  1. Profile ⋯ → Report → They’re pretending to be me or someone else.

Intimate images or blackmail

  1. Report the post and choose the option about private media shared without consent.

Block and protect your account

  1. Settings → Privacy and safety: protect your posts and limit who can reply and message you.

Menus change between app versions. If you can’t find an option, search the Help Centre for “report impersonation” or “report intimate images”. Open X (Twitter) Help Centre ↗

YTYouTube

Report a profile or content

  1. Open the video or channel. On a video, tap ⋯ → Report. On a channel, open the About tab, tap the flag icon, then Report user.
  2. Pick the closest reason and submit.
  3. For comments, tap ⋯ beside the comment, then Report.

Someone pretending to be you

  1. Channel → About → flag icon → Report user → impersonation.

Intimate images or blackmail

  1. Report the video and use the options for harassment or sexual content. Use the privacy complaint process for videos that show you.

Block and protect your account

  1. Studio → Settings: turn on comment moderation, hold potentially inappropriate comments, and add blocked words.

Menus change between app versions. If you can’t find an option, search the Help Centre for “report impersonation” or “report intimate images”. Open YouTube Help Centre ↗

TGTelegram

Report a profile or content

  1. Open the chat, tap the name at the top, then ⋯ → Report.
  2. Choose the reason and submit. You can also report single messages by pressing and holding them.
  3. Block the user from the same menu.

Someone pretending to be you

  1. Report the account, and for groups or channels use ⋯ → Report as well.

Intimate images or blackmail

  1. Report the message or channel and choose the pornography or illegal content reason.

Block and protect your account

  1. Settings → Privacy and Security: set who can see your phone number, last seen and photo to “My Contacts”.

Menus change between app versions. If you can’t find an option, search the Help Centre for “report impersonation” or “report intimate images”. Open Telegram Help Centre ↗

Messages you can copy

Replace the text in [BRACKETS], then paste into the platform’s report form. Keep it short and factual.

Report an impersonating account

Use in the platform’s impersonation form.
Hello,
The account [PROFILE LINK] is pretending to be me, [YOUR NAME]. It uses my photos and name without my permission and is contacting other people as me.
Please review and remove it. I can provide identification through your official form if needed.
Thank you.

Report blackmail or intimate images

Use when someone threatens to share private content, or already did.
Hello,
The account [PROFILE LINK] is threatening to share private images of me unless I pay or send more content. This is blackmail and sharing intimate images without my consent.
Please remove the content and suspend the account urgently, and preserve the account data for law enforcement.
Thank you.

Report harassment

Use for repeated abuse, hate or threats.
Hello,
The account [PROFILE LINK] has repeatedly sent me abusive and threatening messages since [DATE]. I have saved screenshots. Please review the messages, take action on the account, and keep the records available for an official complaint.
Thank you.

Report a hacked account

Use with the platform’s account recovery or support form.
Hello,
My account [USERNAME / EMAIL] was accessed without my permission on or around [DATE]. I can no longer sign in / my recovery details were changed. Please help me recover it and secure it.
Thank you.
