Safety plan

Know who to tell, and tell them fast.

Save up to three trusted people and a ready-made message. In an emergency, one tap opens SMS, WhatsApp or a call with the message already written.

In immediate danger?Call local emergency services now: 15 (police) or 1122 (rescue). Then use the alert below.

1. Your trusted people

Choose people who will answer and keep it private. Saved on this phone only.

2. Your message

Keep it short and clear. Edit it to fit your situation.

3. Send an alert

Tap a person to send your message by the method you choose.

Add at least one trusted person above to enable one-tap alerts.

Who to tell first

If you are not sure, work down this list.

  1. Someone who is physically near you and can be with you tonight.
  2. An adult you trust: a parent, sibling, teacher, counsellor or senior colleague.
  3. A helpline such as 0800-39393, if you can’t think of anyone.
  4. The authorities: NCCIA 1799 or FIA 9911, once your evidence is saved.
